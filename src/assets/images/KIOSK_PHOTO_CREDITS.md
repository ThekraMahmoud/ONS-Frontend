- **Makkah:** `kiosk-makkah.jpg`, “Kaaba, Makkah.jpg”, Wikimedia Commons, author معتز توفيق اغبارية (Moataz Tawfek Egbaria), photograph dated 31 March 2016. CC BY-SA 3.0. The on-screen photo caption includes attribution and license. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kaaba,_Makkah.jpg
- **Madinah:** `kiosk-madinah.jpg`, “MasjidNabawi.jpg”, Wikimedia Commons, author Wurzelgnohm, photograph dated 9 November 2010. CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MasjidNabawi.jpg

The kiosk uses the photograph matching its active kiosk when the configured location is recognizable. Before a device is selected, and when its location cannot be resolved, the welcome screen defaults to the Makkah photograph; a recognized Madinah location uses the Madinah photograph.
